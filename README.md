## About me

I'm Domantas, an independent developer based in Lithuania and the person behind **Builtbyuba**. I build apps and practical tools, taking ideas from an early concept through design, development and improvement.

My focus is mobile app development: creating straightforward experiences that make everyday tasks easier. I enjoy learning by building, refining the details and turning small ideas into products people can use.

## What I'm building toward

- **Android & Google Play** — my current focus is building Android apps and preparing them for release on Google Play.
- **iOS & the Apple App Store** — a future step is bringing my work to iPhone and expanding to the Apple ecosystem.
- **Beyond mobile** — I also work on web apps and automation tools when an idea calls for them.

This GitHub is where I share projects, experiments and the work behind that journey.

## Main projects

### [Candrivo](https://github.com/builtbyuba/Candrivo)

A trading analysis app that turns chart screenshots into structured analysis. Upload a TradingView chart, review market conditions and potential setups, save the analysis to history, or export a Trade Card.

**Built with:** Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Google Gemini

### [InstaDrop](https://github.com/builtbyuba/InstaDrop)

A native Android app for sharing links, previewing media and saving it to your gallery. The project includes download progress, persistent history and a Material 3 interface.

**Built with:** Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Material 3 · Room
